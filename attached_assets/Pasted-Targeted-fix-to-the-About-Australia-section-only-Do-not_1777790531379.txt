Tile five. Time Zones. Stripe Copper.
Body: Australia spans five main time zones. AEST UTC+10: NSW, VIC, QLD, TAS, ACT. AEDT UTC+11: NSW, VIC, TAS, ACT during daylight saving. ACST UTC+9:30: SA and NT. Tile six. Daylight Saving. Stripe Coral.
Body: Daylight saving runs from the first Sunday in October to the first Sunday in April. Clocks go forward one hour in October. Back one hour in April. States that observe it: NSW, VIC, SA, TAS, ACT. States that do not: QLD, WA, NT. Queensland held referendums in 1992 and 2010 and voted no both times.
Source: australia.gov.au.
